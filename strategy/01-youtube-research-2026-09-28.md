*All view counts, velocities, keyword metrics and title scores below come from live vidIQ, TubeAlfred and web pulls run 2026-09-28. vidIQ search-volume figures are vidIQ's modeled global estimates; treat as directional, not census.*

## 1. What's winning right now (every data point traced to a pull)

1. **Model-launch tutorials are the single hottest lane.** In a TubeAlfred popularity search for `claude code tutorial` (past 30 days), the top non-English result — Adrián Sáenz "Dame 34 Minutos y Te Doy 1000 Horas De Conocimiento Sobre Claude" — hit **819,307 views in 3 weeks**, and Jay E/RoboNuggets "Jev will 10x your Claude Code" hit **659,536 views in 6 days**. Multiple GPT-6 Astra / Fable 5.1 / Claude Opus 5.5 videos cleared 400–630k in 2–3 weeks (Shubham Sharma 626,390; Stefan 3D AI 614,192; Rob Shocks "INTENT.MD" 472,510). **Why:** they ride a breakout-keyword spike the moment a model drops, before competition catches up.

2. **"I built X with an AI agent" concrete-outcome tutorials pull 90k–495k.** From `n8n ai agent tutorial` (popularity, 30d): Nate Herk "I Turned GPT-6 Astra Into a 24/7 Stock Trader" = **495,328 views/2wk**; Riley Brown "8 ChatGPT Agents That Do My Work for Me" = **101,515**; Tech With Tim "Build a Local AI Agent in 10 Minutes using Python" = **131,693**. **Why:** a specific, verifiable end-result ("stock trader," "shops the internet for you") is the click driver, not the tool name.

3. **Tiny channels break out hard with the right hook.** vidIQ outliers (`AI agents build automation`, this month): OPENRIG "I Run an AI Civilization in Herdr" = **167,943 views at breakout score 811 from a 2,910-sub channel** (vph 1,130); and "Draw This Before You Build an AI Agent" (Shikha Bahirani, **1,180 subs**) broke out to **25,254 views (breakout 89)**. **Why:** the second one is a whiteboard/architecture **diagram explainer** — directly the format Noirsys's "Noirsystic Pipeline" lends itself to.

4. **Velocity is available to mid-size tech channels.** vidIQ trending (`AI agent build tutorial`, sorted vph, published since Aug 20): SavvyNik "Linux is Wild: AI Agents, Rust Drivers…" was doing **3,080 views/hour** (20,432 views, ~7h old, 107k-sub channel); BMF MEDIA "I Used Kimi AI Agent Swarm to Build a Local Business Workflow" = **930 vph / 56,618 views**. **Why:** news-pegged, fast-turn uploads capture the algorithm's early-velocity window.

5. **AI-doom / AI-safety explainers are proven at scale — but only on big channels.** vidIQ outliers surfaced CBC News "A reasonable person's guide to how AI destroys humanity" = **1,842,792 views** and BBC World Service "Could AI actually wipe out humanity?" = **165,032 views**. **Why it matters for Noirsys:** the appetite is enormous, but see #6.

6. **The "watch an AI actually lie / interpretability" niche is near-empty whitespace.** A TubeAlfred popularity search for `how AI lies interpretability what AI is thinking` (past year) returned almost nothing that worked: the best result was an Anthropic-explainer dub at **3,050 views**, then **1,312 / 528 / 68 / 57 / 34 / 28 / 27 / 14 / 12 views**. **Why it matters:** huge top-funnel demand (see #5 + keyword growth in §2) with essentially zero small-creator supply that *demonstrates it live*. Noirsys's **StrAIght Shot** (linear probes + Jacobian-lens + token-entropy) is one of the only assets that can actually show the internal signal on screen. This is the strongest single opening in the whole dataset.

7. **Faceless AI channels are compounding fast in 2026 (vidIQ channel search, faceless=true, sorted 30-day sub growth):**
   - **BoomAI** — AI-tools, faceless, **65,500 subs, +309% subs / +348% views in 30 days**, created **2026-08-17** (≈6 weeks old), only 9 videos, avg 36k views, ~6-min uploads.
   - **tef** — AI dev, faceless, **144,000 subs, avg 181,007 views per video**, +21,586% subs over 1y.
   - **AI LABS** (autometa.dev) — faceless, **166,000 subs**, run by a software company that "build[s] real products with AI every day" — the closest existing analogue to the Noirsys model.
   - **Universe of AI** — faceless AI-news/models/agents, 31,800 subs, **26 long videos in 30 days** (near-daily cadence, ~10-min each). **Why:** proves both the fast-news-roundup cadence and the "we ship real products" positioning independently work faceless.

8. **Economics favor this niche (WebSearch, 2026 sources).** AI/tech tutorials carry **$8–20 CPM** (finance/AI/coding beat gaming/vlogs 5–30× on CPM); AI documentaries reportedly run **$10–14 RPM**. YouTube's dual-path monetization (10M Shorts views *or* 4,000 watch hours) makes a Shorts arm viable. *(Flagged: CPM/RPM figures are third-party blog estimates, not measured Noirsys data.)*

## 3. Four faceless channel concepts for Noirsys

Shared production spine (grounded in WebSearch 2026 stack): **script (Claude) → voiceover (ElevenLabs cloned voice for hook/CTA, cheaper TTS for the middle 80% if credits matter) → b-roll (screen capture + generated motion graphics) → captioned assembly → Shorts cut via noirpost.live's own VOD→Shorts pipeline.** Note: per third-party reporting on YouTube's Aug-2026 disclosure rules, an AI voice that is a clone of the creator's *own* voice may not require the synthetic-voice label — **verify against YouTube's current policy before relying on it**; noirstudio adds a disclosure line to every description by default regardless. Every channel dogfoods and demos Noirsys tooling.

**Concept 1 — "Noirsys Signal" (AI-news roundup, faceless).**
Format: 6–10-min data-viz news brief + 2–3 Shorts/day. Cadence: daily or 5×/week (matches Universe of AI's proven 26-videos/30-day pace). Fit: pure top-of-funnel reach machine; rides Cluster A launches (opus 5.5, gpt-6 astra, grok). Pipeline: RSS/API → Claude summarizes → templated motion-graphics scenes → TTS → auto-assemble. Near-100% automatable; this is the volume/subscriber engine that feeds the others.

**Concept 2 — "Built by Noirsys" (terminal-cast build logs).**
Format: 10–18-min screen-recording of actually building agents/tools — Claude Code, MCP servers, autonomous loops — shipping to production. Cadence: 2×/week. Fit: this is literally Noirsys's day job (the Noirsystic Pipeline: discover→engineer→verify→deploy→deliver); Code Clerk, StrAIght Shot and noirpost.live are ready-made episodes. Mirrors AI LABS (166k, software-company model). Pipeline: real capture + Claude-written narration + cloned voice + captions. This is the authority/credibility + lead-gen channel.

**Concept 3 — "Glass Box" (interpretability & AI-safety data-viz explainers).**
Format: 8–14-min explainers that *show the model's internals on screen* — probes lighting up, entropy spiking, a jailbreak caught pre-output. Cadence: 1×/week. Fit: the §1.6 whitespace — proven demand (CBC 1.8M, BBC 165k), near-zero demonstrable supply, and StrAIght Shot is the instrument. This is the differentiated flagship. Pipeline: real StrAIght Shot telemetry → animated overlays → scripted narration → TTS.

**Concept 4 — "Autonomous in the Wild" (real-deployment case studies).**
Format: 10–15-min mini-doc explainers of live systems — AFLR (automated newsroom), Code Clerk (AI zoning clerk), Project Tripwire (privacy-first vehicle-ledger). Cadence: 1× every 1–2 weeks. Fit: "evidence-governed autonomous systems" as narrative; leans on the diagram-explainer breakout pattern (§1.3) + case-study demand. Pipeline: diagrams + screen recording + narration; highest craft, lowest cadence.

## 5. Founder-face shortlist — projects worth putting Michael on camera

Ranked by how strongly the data says they'd pop:

1. **StrAIght Shot — "Watch an AI's mind change before it lies."** Single strongest hook in the report. §1.6 shows the topic has 1.8M-view ceiling demand (CBC/BBC) and effectively zero small-creator supply that *demonstrates it live*; keyword momentum is +155% (ai safety) / +203% (ai regulation) / +229% (agi). A named researcher on camera showing linear probes + Jacobian-lens firing in real time is credibility no faceless channel can fake — and it's a live tool, not a think-piece.
2. **Code Clerk — "I let an AI run a county government office."** vidIQ scored these hooks **94–97** (the highest in the set). "AI does real government work" is a concrete, slightly transgressive outcome — exactly the pattern behind the 100k–495k "I built X" winners. Vega on camera = accountability the premise demands.
3. **Project Tripwire — "A privacy-first AI that watches the watchers."** Hook scored **95**. Counter-surveillance + privacy is emotionally charged and politically legible (rides `ai regulation` +203%); a real founder defending the privacy stance on camera is what separates this from generic AI-tool content.
4. **AFLR (autonomous newsroom) — "AI agents ran a real newsroom for 30 days."** Hook scored **97**. "The future of journalism?" is a debate-bait framing; a founder narrating wins/failures earns the trust a faceless voice can't, and doubles as the flagship case study.
5. **"Wasted Compute Theory" / consciousness-assessment research — "The AI research nobody's funding."** Lower ceiling but distinct: original-research explainers (à la the CMU/IBM course content that hit 74k–489k in outliers) build durable authority. Best as an occasional founder-led video essay, not a core cadence.

**Recommendation:** Run Concept 1 (Signal) + Concept 2 (Built by Noirsys) faceless for reach and cadence; make **Concept 3 / StrAIght Shot the founder-face flagship** — it's the rare case where demand is huge, competition is empty, and Noirsys owns the one instrument that proves the claim.
